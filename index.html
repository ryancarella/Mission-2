04 / COMPETITIVE OPPORTUNITIES

A position worth testing.

Strategic interpretations from Mission 1. Proposed features remain untested.

01Hypothesis

Make nutrition easy to track.

Post measured portions and visible calories and macros. This could create a clearer tracking experience for fitness-focused diners.

Boundary: Chipotle already offers nutrition information. A market-wide nutrition gap is not established.

02Proposed offer

Personalize with purpose.

Explore extra protein, varied seasonings, and clearly labeled dietary options. Test a standard bowl and a separately priced extra-protein option.

Boundary: customization already exists at Chipotle and CAVA. No add-on price was researched.

03Preliminary

Start near the local benchmark.

The $13–$15 research range spans the $13.95 sample median. Convenience, satisfying portions, and clear macros could support the value proposition.

Preliminary — Subject to Cost and Demand Analysis. No optimal price has been established.

How to read the numbers

Representative benchmarks: P01, P04, and P07, the three priced chicken-and-rice bowls selected in Mission 1. P10 is a representative salad with no price; it remains visible but is excluded from arithmetic.

All offers: 10 researched records, 9 with numeric prices. These cover different ingredients and portion sizes and are not a like-for-like market benchmark. No separately priced add-ons are present.

Calculations: minimum, maximum, arithmetic mean, and median use finite numeric prices only. Each competitor is counted once. Filters affect the pricing comparison section only; the executive and explorer benchmarks remain fixed and labeled.

Conditions: All records are USD per item, one-time purchases. Tax, delivery, service fees, and extras are excluded. Promotions, regular-price history, and stock availability were not confirmed; unavailable price does not mean out of stock.
